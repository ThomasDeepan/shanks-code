# 01 — Product Specification: Shank's Code

## 1. Executive Summary & Vision
**Shank's Code** is a premier, modern technology education platform and student learning ecosystem. It bridges the gap between academic theory and high-impact software engineering by delivering industry-aligned curricula, hands-on development projects, and a structured, distraction-free digital learning experience.

The platform serves two primary user spaces:
1. **Public Learning Platform**: Discovery portal for prospective learners, working professionals, and university students seeking high-caliber training in software development, cloud systems, and applied engineering.
2. **Authenticated Student Learning Portal**: A dedicated, focused learning management hub where enrolled students track their academic progress, consume structured multimedia lessons, complete timed technical assessments, monitor attendance, and access their class schedules.

---

## 2. Brand Identity & Visual Philosophy
Shank's Code projects an authoritative, modern, and trustworthy aesthetic. It rejects dated, cluttered portal designs and gimmicky dark-mode visual fatigue in favor of clarity, performance, and ergonomic readability.

- **Interface Philosophy**: **Light-First**. Clean white surfaces, soft neutral backdrops, and disciplined contrast.
- **Primary Brand Color**: **Deep Navy** (`#0F172A`, `#1E293B`) representing stability, rigor, and engineering excellence.
- **Interactive & Accent Color**: **Electric Blue** (`#2563EB`, `#1D4ED8`) for primary calls-to-action, progress highlights, and focused interactive states.
- **Semantic Colors**: Emerald (`#059669`) for positive achievements and completion, Amber (`#D97706`) for upcoming deadlines or in-progress states, Rose (`#E11D48`) for critical alerts and exam timer urgency.
- **Visual Restraint**: No unnecessary dark mode, no theme toggle switches, no excessive glassmorphism, no neon glows, and no heavy visual animations that detract from focused learning.

---

## 3. Target Audience & Personas
1. **Aspiring Software Engineers**: Beginners to intermediate coders seeking disciplined, end-to-end full-stack and systems engineering mastery.
2. **Career Upskillers**: Experienced professionals transitioning into modern cloud, backend, or full-stack roles requiring structured validation of skills.
3. **Enrolled Academy Students**: Active students who require daily access to video lessons, interactive quizzes, attendance records, and schedule timelines.

---

## 4. Functional Reference Policy (Edutantr)
The Edutantr platform is utilized strictly as a **functional and workflow reference** to comprehend:
- Essential EdTech user journeys and mental models.
- Standard curriculum hierarchy (Program -> Course -> Module -> Lesson).
- Student assessment flow (Timed Exam -> Questions -> Submission -> Performance Breakdown).
- Academic record keeping (Attendance calendar, weekly schedule).

> [!CAUTION]
> **Zero Visual or Asset Replication**: Under no circumstances should Edutantr’s visual identity, logo, styling, layout quirks, color palette, or typography be mirrored. Shank's Code must maintain an entirely proprietary, distinct, high-fidelity UI and component library.

---

## 5. Core Value Propositions
- **Curated Technical Depth**: Clear modular roadmaps without fluff.
- **Measurable Progress**: Continuous evaluation via timed assessments and granular topic-level performance metrics.
- **Operational Clarity**: Transparent schedules, attendance visibility, and direct learning milestones.
